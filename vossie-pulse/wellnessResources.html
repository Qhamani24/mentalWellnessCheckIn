Relaxation Exercises

Try these calming techniques to reduce stress and restore balance:

  • Deep Breathing: Inhale slowly for 4 seconds, hold for 4, exhale for 6. Repeat 5 times.
  • Progressive Muscle Relaxation: Tense each muscle group for 5 seconds, then release. Start from toes up to shoulders.
  • Guided Imagery: Close your eyes and imagine a peaceful place (beach, forest, or garden). Focus on the sounds and sensations.
  • Mindful Stretching: Gentle neck rolls, shoulder shrugs, and slow arm stretches while focusing on your breath.
  • Music Therapy: Play calming instrumental music or nature sounds for 10 minutes to reset your mood.
